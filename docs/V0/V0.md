# Product V0: Sakhaa Forge

## Purpose

Product V0 is the first product built and released. Sakhaa Forge is a complete standalone
application that learns an approved brand, discovers or reuses a viral structure,
generates and selects scripts, produces an avatar-led video through HeyGen, composes the
final branded video, collects approval, schedules and publishes it, verifies the
audience-facing post, and records cost and lineage.

V0 is complete only when it operates with Product V1 and Product V2 absent.

## Initial Target

- Initial customer profile: India-first real-estate brands and internal operators.
- Primary job: produce an approved, published short-form marketing video through a
  repeatable workflow rather than a one-off manual service.
- Primary output: a verified published post plus complete brand, blueprint, script,
  generation, review, billing and publication lineage.
- Reference acceptance journey: one real-estate client completes the entire workflow
  without manual intervention inside the pipeline.
- Product claim boundary: V0 uses observed structural patterns to assist production; it
  does not guarantee virality, reach, conversion or causal performance improvement.

## Required User Journey

```text
Client URL and approved brand assets
  -> brand candidates extracted
  -> human approves exact brand profile
  -> user selects existing blueprint or new viral extraction
  -> thumbnail and video structure deciphered
  -> formula and provider-neutral director prompt produced
  -> 10-20 scripts generated and evaluated
  -> one exact script selected
  -> cost estimated and creator credits reserved
  -> HeyGen produces the avatar-led video
  -> AE plan validated and final branded MP4 rendered
  -> internal/client review approves the exact final version
  -> approved post scheduled and submitted
  -> audience-facing viewer verifies account, media and visibility
  -> success notification sent once
  -> credits captured once and complete lineage retained
```

## Functional Requirements

### Identity and Workspaces

- Supabase Auth-backed users, workspaces, memberships and V0 production roles.
- Server-side authorization for every tenant resource and sensitive action.
- Workspace-scoped audit, storage, queues, cache keys and database rows.
- Permissions follow [V0_PERMISSIONS.md](V0_PERMISSIONS.md).

### Brand Detail Extraction

- Accept website URLs, brand-guideline files, logos, product images and existing copy.
- Crawl only permitted pages and block SSRF, unsafe redirects and oversized downloads.
- Extract logo, colors, fonts, tone, offers, USPs, CTAs, audiences and prohibited claims.
- Preserve source evidence and confidence for every candidate.
- Require human approval before any candidate becomes production brand truth.

### Viral Discovery and Blueprinting

- Present an explicit choice between an existing blueprint and new viral discovery.
- Search and rank Xpoz candidates for the selected niche.
- Store immutable metric snapshots and source/right warnings.
- Process thumbnail, metadata, scenes, transcript, keyframes, vision and OCR separately.
- Merge them into an immutable structural blueprint with formula slots and replacement
  instructions.
- Block or expose low-confidence states; never silently invent missing stages.
- V0 stores reusable blueprints. Template clustering across many blueprints is deferred
  to V1.

### Virality Script System

- Generate 10-20 brand- and formula-constrained script variants.
- Evaluate hook strength, timing, pattern interrupts, CTA, claims, captions and tone.
- Preserve every variant, evaluation, model/prompt version and human decision.
- Require one immutable selected-script identity before paid generation.
- Provide the approved default formula when no extracted blueprint is used.

### Avatars and HeyGen Generation

- Support generic, reusable brand-ambassador and consented real-person avatars.
- Record likeness and voice scope, evidence, expiry and revocation.
- Use HeyGen as the required primary V0 route.
- Show a versioned cost estimate and maximum authorization before submission.
- Reserve credits and persist a provider operation before network I/O.
- Treat timeout after possible provider acceptance as `unknown` and reconcile it.
- Never blindly resubmit an uncertain paid operation.

### Composition

- Convert user direction into a versioned AE timeline plan.
- Validate assets, duration, captions, effects, fonts, timing and output constraints.
- Render a retained, fingerprinted 9:16 final MP4 with thumbnail and captions.
- Keep composition revisions linked to immutable upstream inputs.

### Review

- Provide video, thumbnail, caption and script preview.
- Support comments, requested changes, approval, rejection and version history.
- Bind every decision to the exact final-video version and actor.
- Prevent scheduling or publication of unapproved or superseded media.

### Calendar, Publishing and Verification

- Implement Meta first, YouTube/Shorts second, and manual export fallback.
- Preserve account, platform, schedule, caption, media identity and approval.
- Publish idempotently and store the platform post ID and public URL.
- Verify the live post independently after provider/API acknowledgement.
- Confirm target account, media identity, caption, visibility and publish time.
- Send completion only after `published_verified`; retries must not duplicate posts or
  notifications.

### Credits, Payments and Cost

- Support creator-credit wallets, Razorpay for India and Stripe for international use.
- Use append-only purchase, reserve, capture, release, refund and adjustment entries.
- Use integer minor units or provider-native credit micros, never floating-point money.
- Charge using an approved price version and show the estimate before confirmation.
- Capture or release exactly once and reconcile the provider total to the V0 ledger.

### Performance and Lineage

- Retain brand profile, blueprint, formula, prompt, script, avatar, provider, composition,
  review, publication and cost ancestry.
- Store immutable publication and performance snapshots with observation windows.
- Produce export-ready events, but do not require V1 or V2 delivery for V0 acceptance.

## Technical Requirements

- Web: Next.js and TypeScript.
- Domain API: NestJS with Fastify; sole domain API and PostgreSQL writer.
- Database/Auth: Supabase PostgreSQL and Supabase Auth.
- ORM/migrations: Prisma is the sole schema and migration owner.
- Heavy SQL: reviewed parameterized modules only under the project allowlist.
- Jobs: BullMQ with managed Redis for wake-up delivery; PostgreSQL is canonical.
- Workers: private Python/media workers without PostgreSQL or Redis credentials.
- Storage: private Backblaze B2 quarantine, clean-media and private-artifact areas.
- Providers: adapters for generation, payment, publishing, crawling and AI services.
- Region: India-first control plane; benchmark the cross-region B2 path.

## Build Gates

| Gate | Required outcome | Blocking evidence |
|---|---|---|
| V0-G0 Foundation | Auth, tenancy, RLS, schema, queues, B2, telemetry and local simulators work | Isolation tests, migration validation, local startup and restore evidence |
| V0-G1 Brand | Approved exact brand profile can be created from URL/uploads | Crawl safety tests, candidate evidence and approval audit |
| V0-G2 Blueprint | Existing and new-discovery paths produce the same downstream contract | Fixture artifacts, failure states and immutable blueprint record |
| V0-G3 Scripts | Tournament preserves all candidates and selects one evaluated script | Prompt/model versions, evaluations and selection audit |
| V0-G4 Generation | HeyGen completes with exactly-once reservation/capture/release | Estimate, provider operation, callback/reconciliation and ledger proof |
| V0-G5 Composition | Valid AE plan produces retained final branded MP4 | Plan validation, media hash and render lineage |
| V0-G6 Review | Exact final version receives auditable approval | Review decision and superseded-version rejection tests |
| V0-G7 Publishing | Approved post is scheduled, published and audience-verified | Platform ID, public URL, verification evidence and notification ID |
| V0-G8 Production | Reference real-estate journey succeeds with V1/V2 absent | Full trace, screenshots, IDs, hashes, ledger reconciliation and recovery report |

## Release-Blocking Invariants

- No unapproved brand profile enters generation.
- No cross-workspace read, write, signed URL, queue, cache or callback succeeds.
- No paid provider submission or ledger capture happens twice.
- No uncertain provider timeout triggers blind resubmission.
- No unconsented avatar, voice, media or brand asset is used.
- No review approval applies to a different or superseded video version.
- No post is called successful before audience-facing verification.
- No V0 workflow requires V1, V2, TRIBEv2, HCP or A-Q.
- No secret enters browser code, logs, prompts or retained artifacts.

## Required Acceptance Evidence

- One complete production-shaped real-estate run.
- Screenshots of brand approval, blueprint choice, script selection, generation estimate,
  review approval, calendar and verified post.
- Stable IDs and hashes connecting every artifact.
- Provider request/callback/reconciliation evidence.
- Credit purchase, reservation, capture/release and reconciliation evidence.
- Cross-tenant, duplicate, timeout, crash-window and restore test reports.
- India-to-B2 latency/cost benchmark.
- Operator runbook and rollback rehearsal.
- V1 and V2 disabled or absent during the final V0 acceptance run.

## Explicitly Deferred to V1

- structural template clustering at useful volume;
- automated isolated-variable A/B testing;
- broader generation and publishing providers;
- improved avatar, composition and media quality;
- higher-scale queues and operational automation;
- mature signed exports and the stable V2 integration contract;
- execution of approved V2 successor briefs.

## Explicitly Outside V0

- TRIBEv2 inference, HCP-MMP1 mapping and A-Q evidence;
- Sakhaa scoring, comparison and recommendations;
- calibration, datasets, model training and model governance;
- V2 scientific, predictive or causal claims;
- any requirement that V0 wait for V1 or V2.

## V0 Completion Rule

V0 is not complete because individual components exist. It is complete only when every
gate above passes together in the reference journey, all release-blocking invariants hold,
and the required evidence is retained.

Technical completion authorizes a controlled pilot, not unrestricted commercial launch.
Before external launch, the founder must approve a measured pilot scorecard covering
time to first approved video, approval/revision rate, provider and infrastructure cost,
operator minutes per verified post, repeat usage and customer willingness to pay.
