**Purpose:** Collect the minimum evidence required to extract and approve customer brand
truth for V0 production.

This template is for the customer's brand. It is separate from the Virality Creator
Engine's own `PROJECT_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`.

## 6. Audience

For each approved audience:

- audience name;
- location;
- life/business context;
- purchase or funnel stage;
- key need;
- key objection;
- affordability/eligibility constraint, if relevant and lawful;

and approved. V0 must not infer protected traits.

## 9. Claims and Evidence

For each claim:

| Claim | Status | Evidence | Evidence expiry | Required wording/disclaimer |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Status is one of:

- `approved`;
- `approved_with_disclaimer`;
- `prohibited`;
- `pending_evidence`.

Examples requiring evidence include regulatory registration, delivery dates, amenities,
distance/travel time, price, inventory, awards and return/appreciation statements.
