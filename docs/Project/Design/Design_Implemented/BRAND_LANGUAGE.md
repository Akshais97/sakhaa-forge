# Brand language

Status: design-language guidance. Product copy remains governed by `PROJECT_CONTENT_AND_LANGUAGE_GUIDE.md`.

## Brand idea

Sakhaa Forge is a premium creative intelligence studio for India-first real-estate video production. The product should sound precise, calm and commercially aware, not magical or speculative.

## Approved language direction

- Creative intelligence over generic generation.
- Built from approved brand truth and observed structural patterns.
- Evidence-backed decisions, retained lineage and verified publication.
- Real-estate specificity: project launches, site visits, walkthrough cuts, proof overlays, verified social posts and campaign history.

## Prohibited language direction

- Guaranteed virality, reach, leads, conversion, attention, emotion or causal improvement.
- "Magic", "instant", "fully automated with no review", "scientifically proven to convert".
- Internal implementation names as public value proposition.
- Overly technical landing copy that foregrounds adapters, queues, database state or provider internals.

## Tone

- Calm, precise, premium, evidence-first.
- Use active verbs: approve, select, generate, reserve, review, publish, verify, inspect.
- Use uncertainty words honestly: unknown, estimated, observed, low confidence, blocked.
