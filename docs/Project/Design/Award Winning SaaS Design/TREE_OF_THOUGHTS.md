# Tree of Thoughts

This is a documented design decision artifact, not private reasoning. It records branches, tradeoffs, eliminations and selected rules.

## Root design problem

Make Sakhaa Forge feel like a premium creative intelligence studio while remaining a truthful system of record for brand approval, viral source evidence, paid generation, review, publication verification, lineage and recovery.

## Core product truth

V0 starts with approved brand truth and ends only at audience-verified publication with retained evidence. It cannot guarantee virality, reach, conversion, attention, emotion or causal performance.

## Branches considered

### Landing page direction

- Option A: friendly light SaaS. Clear and approachable, but risks generic AI SaaS softness.
- Option B: dark cinematic evidence lab. Stronger premium/video fit, but can overpromise if scoring visuals imply certainty.
- Option C: editorial Open Design collage. Memorable, but less directly tied to real-estate video production and evidence.

Selected: B as base, with A's user-friendly real-estate clarity and C's template discipline, not its editorial identity.

### App design direction

- Option A: creative tool dashboard. Energetic and visual, but risks fake metrics and irreversible-action confusion.
- Option B: operational command centre. Trustworthy and stateful, but could feel dry.
- Option C: hybrid studio instrument. Media is central, evidence and cost surround it.

Selected: C.

### Visual style branches

- Swiss/Modernist grid: selected as structure.
- Dark mode first: selected for media and premium feel.
- Luxury minimal and Corporate Professional: selected for trust.
- Glassmorphism and Gradient Modern: limited to hero/product composites.
- Art Deco and Neo-Geo: limited to fine linework and geometric proof motifs.
- Neumorphic/Skeuomorphic: limited to high-value media/score objects, not app chrome.
- Calm/Scandinavian: spacing influence only.
- Organic/Fluid: controlled background motion only, no random blobs.

### Trust and evidence branches

- Decorative proof metrics: rejected.
- Evidence chips, hashes and timestamps: selected.
- Lineage trail always available from exact artifacts: selected.

### Scoring, neurology and feedback branches

- V0 product copy cannot present V2 scoring as shipped.
- Design specs may define future-compatible components, but label implementation status and uncertainty.
- Scores must show source, confidence and limitation when implemented.

## Selected direction

Award-winning direction: "Cinematic evidence studio". The landing page shows the product as a premium video creation control surface. The app is a calm Studio Instrument with exact state, source evidence, cost transparency and verified publication.
