# Landing page master spec

## Hero

Show Sakhaa Forge as a proof-first real-estate video creation engine. First viewport must include product name, 9:16 video/proof composite, approved brand truth, viral source card, cost/review/verified post indicators and one primary CTA.

## Sections

1. Approved brand truth.
2. Viral discovery with source evidence and rights warning.
3. Blueprint-to-video flow.
4. Director room: script, scene, formula and prompt decisions.
5. Cost, avatar safety and provider transparency.
6. Human review, scheduling and audience verification.
7. Lineage and performance evidence archive.
8. CTA with claim-safe language.

## Style

Dark premium base, cinematic product objects, precise grid, restrained accent, tactile media frame, evidence chips, tabular metrics and linework.

## Copy boundaries

No guaranteed virality, reach, conversion, attention, emotion or causal performance. Use observed, estimated, approved, verified, retained and unknown precisely.
