# Sakhaa Forge landing image brief

Source basis: `docs/V0/V0.md`, `docs/V0/V0_INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/V0/V0_SCREEN_AND_STATE_INVENTORY.md`, `docs/Project/DESIGN.md`, `docs/Project/Design/PROJECT_CONTENT_AND_LANGUAGE_GUIDE.md`, and `docs/Project/Design/templates/open-design-landing/`.

These are image placement briefs only. Do not use Open Design images. Do not create or imply guaranteed virality, reach, conversion, attention, emotion, scientific prediction, or fully automated publication without review.

## Image 01 - Hero product scene

First-viewport product scene. Show a premium 9:16 real-estate video preview beside a calm command-centre interface. Include visible evidence cues: approved brand profile, viral candidate snapshot, credit reservation, review status and audience verification. The feeling should follow the Open Design editorial composition: numbered artifact, paper texture, precise annotations and strong product signal.

## Image 02 - Brand truth evidence

Show brand intake becoming approved truth. Include website/source cards, uploaded logo or property imagery, extracted colour/type/tone candidates, confidence/source evidence, locked rules and a human-approved profile version. The image should communicate that production cannot start from unapproved brand material.

## Image 03 - Viral candidate card

Show a real-estate viral discovery candidate card. Include thumbnail/media preview, source identity, observed platform metrics, snapshot date and rights warning. Make the rights warning visible but calm. Avoid showing actual third-party platform logos unless licensed.

## Image 04 - Blueprint timeline

Show the selected candidate translated into thumbnail deciphering, OCR, transcript, scenes, formula slots and director prompt. Use a cinematic timeline and numbered stages. Show partial/blocked stage capability without implying fake progress percentages.

## Image 05 - Script tournament

Show 10-20 script variants as a comparison workspace. Include evaluation rows for hook, timing, CTA, brand rules and eligibility, with one immutable selected script. Keep the copy short and non-hype.

## Image 06 - Cost and provider state

Show the paid generation confirmation and provider operation state. Include estimated cost, maximum authorisation, reserved credits, price version, provider operation, and `Unknown - checking` reconciliation. The image must distinguish provider acceptance from final success.

## Image 07 - Review and verification

Show exact final-video review and publication verification. Include 9:16 final video, final-video hash/version, comment/approval state, platform account check, media identity check, caption check, visibility check and final `Published and verified` state.

## Image 08 - Lineage archive

Show retained lineage and ledger evidence. Include brand profile, blueprint, formula, selected script, avatar, provider operation, final video, review decision, calendar post, audience verification, performance snapshot and credit ledger entries as connected immutable objects.

## Global image rules

- Use Sakhaa-owned or explicitly licensed imagery only.
- No Open Design assets, screenshots, logos or marketing text.
- Real-estate visuals should feel India-first and premium without becoming generic stock imagery.
- UI details must use canonical V0 terms and status language.
- Do not render signed URLs, secrets, raw provider payloads, private object keys or protected cross-workspace identifiers.
- Prefer editorial artifact framing from the Open Design template: numbered labels, annotations, warm paper surfaces, precise grid, and restrained coral/mustard accents.
