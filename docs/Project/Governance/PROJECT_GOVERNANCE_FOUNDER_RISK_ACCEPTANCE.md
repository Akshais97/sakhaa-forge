# Founder Assumptions and Risk Acceptance

**Decision date:** 2026-06-11

This record separates decisions the company is entitled to make from facts that must be
measured or legally classified by external evidence.

## Accepted Founder Decisions

1. Phase 1 uses TRIBEv2 only for internal, noncommercial research and product development.
2. The initial segment is in-house performance-marketing and creative teams.
3. The initial value thesis is an audience-taste simulation that helps choose the next
   short-form video revision before another render or campaign test.
4. Executive sponsorship is sufficient to fund and build the internal pilot.
5. Willingness-to-pay research is not a prerequisite for the internal pilot.
6. External billing, production SLO proof, and publication-specific rights workflows are
   delivery gates, not prerequisites for writing the Phase 1 application.
7. Phase 1 will use authorized internal or commissioned media. Likeness, voice, and
   publication attestations become mandatory before external generation or posting.
8. The company accepts the cost and schedule risk of investigating a proprietary student
   model in Phase 2.

## Assertions That Remain Evidence Questions

- Whether A-Q indices predict campaign outcomes.
- Whether recommendations cause improvement.
- Whether external customers will pay at a profitable price.
- Whether a specific teacher-output or distillation process is outside or inside rights
  controlled by source licenses, contracts, copyright, database, privacy, or publicity law.
- Whether the chosen infrastructure meets actual cost, throughput, and reliability needs.

These questions do not block Phase 1 construction. They control claim language, model
promotion, external posting, and commercial launch.

## Risk-Acceptance Rule

The founder may accept product, schedule, and investment risk. The founder cannot make a
security breach, third-party right, scientific result, or regulatory obligation cease to
exist by declaration. Those items use the gates in `PROJECT_GOVERNANCE_COMMERCIAL_READINESS.md`.
