# Commercial Readiness Methodology

Commercial readiness is evaluated separately from software completeness.

## Gate A: Internal Product

- The end-to-end workflow works for authorized internal teams.
- Users complete the decision workflow without specialist intervention.
- Costs and paid actions are visible and bounded.
- Security, rights, audit, export, and deletion policies are implemented.

## Gate B: Repeatable Value

- Multiple independent campaigns show reduced approval time or paid iteration cost.
- Index and recommendation claims use the evidence tier in the claims register.
- Baselines and uncertainty are reported.
- The product has a repeatable operating procedure and named support owner.

## Gate C: External Pilot

- A defined external segment accepts the workflow.
- Pricing hypothesis is tested through paid or contractually committed pilots.
- Legal review covers foundational models, outputs, datasets, generated media, privacy,
  and provider terms.
- Security test, backup restore, incident process, quotas, and support process pass.

## Gate D: SaaS Launch

- Billing, entitlements, taxes, cancellation, refunds, and reconciliation work.
- Production SLOs have measured staging/pilot baselines and error budgets.
- Unit economics meet the approved margin threshold.
- Demand, retention, support burden, and provider concentration are acceptable.

## Scoring Rule

Plans receive credit for explicit decisions and guardrails. Readiness scores receive
credit only for implemented and observed evidence. Founder or executive conviction is a
valid reason to fund a pilot, but it is not a substitute for measured demand or margin.

Accordingly, founder sponsorship clears the internal build decision. Paid-pilot and
margin evidence remain gates only for external pricing and SaaS launch.
