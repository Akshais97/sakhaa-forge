# Privacy and Rights Methodology

## Scope

This methodology covers uploaded media, scripts, voices, likenesses, generated assets,
campaign outcomes, derived features, teacher outputs, datasets, and trained models.

## Phase-Based Requirements

### Internal Research

- Record the source and permitted purpose of every dataset and model.
- Do not publish, sell, or expose TRIBEv2 as a commercial runtime.
- Keep research outputs access-controlled and label scientific limitations.
- Record whether media is owned, commissioned, licensed, public-domain, or otherwise
  authorized for the intended internal use.

### External Generation or Posting

Before submission or publication, require recorded authority for uploaded media, script,
voice, likeness, brand assets, and platform use. Store evidence reference, scope, actor,
date, expiry, and revocation state.

### Customer Data and Model Training

Workspace policy must state whether customer data may be used for service operation,
evaluation, or model training. Training eligibility is separate from permission to
process data for the requested service.

## Rights Decision Record

Every source receives:

- source and owner;
- governing license, contract, platform terms, or permission;
- allowed internal, evaluation, training, distillation, publication, and commercial uses;
- attribution and notice requirements;
- geographic, duration, and downstream restrictions;
- revocation/deletion behavior;
- legal owner and review date.

## Important Constraint

A distilled model having different weights does not by itself prove that source-license,
contract, copyright, privacy, publicity, or data-use obligations disappeared. The release
decision must be based on the governing rights and training process, not visual or
numerical dissimilarity alone.

## Data Minimization and Retention

- Collect only fields needed for the documented purpose.
- Separate raw media, derived features, outcomes, and audit evidence into retention classes.
- Expire provider URLs and scratch artifacts quickly.
- Make dataset manifests reproducible so revoked or deleted records can be located.
- Define whether deletion requires retraining, unlearning, future exclusion, or only raw
  artifact removal, based on the governing policy and law.

## Gate Evidence

Internal pilot readiness requires a completed source inventory. External posting requires
asset and likeness/voice authority. Customer training requires workspace policy and
dataset eligibility. Commercial release requires a legal decision on every foundational
model and dataset category.
