# Privacy and Data Retention

## Data Classes

- Account and workspace data.
- Brand and campaign confidential information.
- Uploaded and generated media.
- Reviewer comments and decisions.
- Campaign outcome and performance data.
- Model artifacts and derived features.

## Principles

Collect only data needed for scoring, review, generation, validation, and agreed training.
Training use requires a documented legal basis and workspace policy. Do not infer or store
protected traits.

## Retention

Retention is configurable by workspace and artifact class. Temporary provider URLs and
scratch caches expire quickly. Approved reports and lineage may outlive raw caches.
Deletion requests remove binaries and personal data while retaining only legally required,
minimized audit records.

## Export

Workspace owners can export campaigns, revisions, reports, outcomes, recommendations,
and audit records in documented formats.

Rights classification, external-posting requirements, training-use policy, and source
decision records follow `PROJECT_SECURITY_PRIVACY_AND_RIGHTS_METHODOLOGY.md`.
